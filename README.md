# 🏨Hotel-Sales-Performance-Analysis

Live Dashboard:📸[https://github.com/Sophie256A/Hotel-Sales-Performance-Analysis/blob/main/Hotel%20Dashboard%20PDF.png]| Tool:🛠️ Power BI, Excel, Power Query, DAX

## 📌Project Overview
This is an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing hotel sales performance for 2024. 
The project transforms raw booking data into actionable business insights to help management understand revenue drivers, customer behavior, and seasonal trends.

## 🎯Business Problem
Hotel management needed to answer:
-What is driving our $46.8M revenue?
-Which room types, meal plans, and customer segments are most profitable?
-Are there seasonal trends we should prepare for?

## 📊 Key KPIs Tracked
-Revenue: $46.8M
-Discounts: $3.5M
-Total Customers: 6K
-Nights Booked: 52K
-Avg. Daily Rate (ADR): $905.3

## 🔍 Key Insights
-Room Type: Studio rooms are the top revenue driver with $21.1M, significantly outperforming Royal Suite ($4.0M).
-Meal Plan: SC (66.02% - $30.9M) is the most preferred meal plan, while FB is the least.
-Customer Segment: Corporate clients generate the highest revenue ($16.5M), followed closely by Family ($15.6M) and Individual ($14.6M) — a balanced segment mix.
-Seasonality: Revenue is highly volatile. Peaks in March ($5.0M) and July ($5.1M) with sharp drops in February ($3.2M) and April ($2.8M).

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
-Power BI Desktop for visualization
-Power Query for data cleaning & transformation
-DAX: SUM, SUMX, CALCULATE, AVERAGE, Measures for ADR, Revenue 
-FiltersFeatures: Slicers for Month, Hotel Name, Distribution Channel, and State | Drill-downs by MonthName & Room Type

-Meal Plan

-Customer Segment
-Interactive filters for dynamic analysisClean, executive-friendly layout

## 💡 What I Learned
This project taught me the importance of the Law of Use — using DAX like SUMX vs SUM correctly to avoid inaccurate ADR calculations, and turning raw bookings into business stories, not just charts.
